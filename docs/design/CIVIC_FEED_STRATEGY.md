# Civic Feed Strategy

## Purpose
Define the long-term vision and beta implementation direction for the CivicMarket civic feed.

## Vision
The CivicMarket civic feed helps residents understand what local government is doing before it affects their lives.

The feed transforms:
- city agendas
- zoning documents
- public notices
- government meetings
- infrastructure updates
- election activity

into a personalized stream of local civic intelligence.

## Core Principles
- Local impact first
- Plain-language government
- Personalized civic awareness
- Nonpartisan utility
- Actionable information

## Feed Content Categories
- Government decisions
- Development & zoning
- Infrastructure
- Public safety
- Elections & representation
- Civic opportunities

## Personalization
Feed relevance should use:
- district
- neighborhood
- followed topics
- Civic DNA
- engagement behavior
- proximity to projects/issues

## Beta Architecture
Government Sources
↓
Scraping / RSS / APIs
↓
Structured Civic Events Database
↓
AI Summary Generation
↓
Admin Review Queue
↓
Published Civic Feed

## Beta AI Rules
AI may:
- summarize
- classify
- organize
- simplify government language

AI may not:
- publish autonomously
- fabricate information
- generate partisan persuasion
- publish unreviewed political claims

## Strategic Goal
Transform CivicMarket from:
- an election-cycle utility

into:
- a year-round civic awareness platform
